# 001 — Labeling schema needs a not_applicable state, not just disclosed/absent/ambiguous

**Date:** 2026-08-06

**Question:** After running `pull_arxiv.py` + `triage_gold_set.py` on the
gold set, 42 of 50 papers had 0 or 1 checklist elements with any keyword
match, out of a possible 5. Is this a broken regex, or a real property of
the corpus?

**Method:** Distribution check across all 50 papers (count of blank
elements per paper): 25 papers at 5/5 blank, 17 at 4/5, rest spread
across 1-3. Then manually read the full text of one 5/5-blank paper
(2608.02311v1, "AI Governance for Institutional Readiness in Finance")
to check whether the checklist elements were genuinely present but
missed by the regex, or genuinely absent from the paper's content.

**What was found:** The paper is a governance-framework paper. Its one
empirical section is a case study of a discretionary, human-run hedge
fund (explicitly stated in-text: "positions are set by human portfolio
managers on a discretionary, thesis-driven basis, not generated by a
retrained policy or an autonomous agent"). There is no trained model,
no backtest, no train/test split of any kind. All five checklist
elements are structurally inapplicable to this paper, not omitted from
an otherwise-relevant methodology section.

**Rejected framing:** Initially treated the 42/50 low-match count as
purely a regex-coverage problem (missing phrasings). That's still an
open item (see ISSUES.md) and needs separate spot-checks on papers that
DO look like backtests. But at least some, possibly a large share, of
the low-match papers are low-match because they were never backtest
papers to begin with -- conflating "should have disclosed and didn't"
with "nothing to disclose" would corrupt the Phase 1 baseline's meaning
before it's even built.

**Result:** Added a fourth label state, `not_applicable`, alongside
disclosed / absent / ambiguous, in the gold-set CSV schema
(`pull_arxiv.py`). A paper only gets scored absent on an element if it
IS the kind of paper where that element would normally appear (i.e. it
reports empirical/backtested trading results) and the disclosure is
missing. Papers with no empirical backtest content at all get
not_applicable across the board, and are excluded from Phase 1's
absent-rate denominator, not folded into it.

**Still open:** Need an explicit rule (not just per-paper judgment) for
what counts as "this paper reports empirical/backtested results" versus
not, so the not_applicable call is itself consistent across the 50
papers and not just a case-by-case decision. Revisit after a few more
papers are labeled and a pattern in the judgment calls becomes visible.

**Reproduction:** `python src/pull_arxiv.py --max-results 500 --gold-size 50 --seed 42`
then manually read https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.02311v1, section 5.6.
